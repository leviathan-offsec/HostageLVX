# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be direct. Assume good faith. Argue about the finding, not the person.

## Expected

**Specific over general.** "This fingerprint matches 41 hosts and the body
response is `The specified bucket does not exist`" is useful. "This tool is
broken" is not.

**Curiosity is the point.** Nobody here is paid to review your PR. Asking why
something works the way it does is the normal way to participate, not an
imposition.

**Publishing your negative results.** A writeup about what did not work, with
the numbers, is worth more here than another win. This project runs on that
idea and it applies to how we treat each other too.

## Not acceptable

**Harassment of any kind.** Personal attacks, slurs, sustained disruption.

**Publishing other people's private information.** Do not paste API keys,
tokens, real customer data, or infrastructure you do not own into an issue, a
PR, or a log. Redact and say that you redacted.

**Unauthorised testing discussion as a how-to.** You may discuss findings in
systems you own or have written permission to test. You may not post a
walkthrough aimed at systems you do not.

**Bad faith argument.** If you are going to disagree, engage with the argument.
Reposting because you dislike the conclusion is not engagement.

## Scope

This applies in issues, PRs, discussions, and anywhere someone is representing
this project. It also applies to private messages about the project.

## Enforcement

Report to **conduct@leviathan.ac**. I will read it, and I will act on it.

Responses, in escalating order: a private word, a public correction, a temporary
ban, a permanent ban. Which one depends on what happened, not on how important
the person thinks they are.

I will keep the reporter's identity out of any public thread unless they want
their name attached.

## Scope of my own enforcement

I am one person and I will get things wrong. If you think I enforced this
badly, email me directly and say so plainly, and I will read it even if you are
not the person I would normally take criticism from.
